  1. Pick the moment

    Paste a YouTube link and say where the part you want starts and ends.

Questions

Is this a downloader?

No. It reads only the window you choose, at most {{MAX_SECONDS}} seconds, while the video plays in your own browser, and turns it into a new vertical clip with the source named on it and linked in the caption.

Does it post for me?

No. It opens the Status composer in your WhatsApp Web with the clip and caption in place. Sending is always your press.

Is it affiliated with YouTube, Google, WhatsApp or Meta?

No. {{NAME}} is an independent experiment. Those names are used only to say what it works with, and they belong to their owners.

What do you keep about me?

Your Google name and email, your request, your last few clips, and daily counts that keep the limits. Never your WhatsApp messages. The privacy page has all of it, and you can delete your account from inside {{NAME}} at any time.

I made a video and want a clip of it removed.

Write to {{CONTACT}} with the link. The clip is removed, and the account that made it can be suspended.

Why is it by invitation?

It is an experiment on a small server. Letting people in by hand keeps it working for the people using it, and keeps it at a person's pace.
