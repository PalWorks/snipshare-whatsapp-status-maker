Privacy

Last updated 25 October 2026

{{NAME}} is an experimental service run by {{OPERATOR}}. This page says what it keeps about you, why, for how long, and who else is involved. Questions go to {{CONTACT}}.

What is kept

  • Your Google account's name, email address, account id and the address of its photo, when you sign in or sign up with Google. They say who you are and show your face on your own pages; nothing else is read from your Google account.
  • What you wrote when asking for access: your name, email, what you will use it for and how you heard of it.
  • Your sessions: one cookie per browser, stored on the server only as a fingerprint, so a stolen database cannot be used to sign in as you, with the kind of browser and system it came from ("Chrome on Linux") and when it was last used, so you can recognise and sign out a browser in your account.
  • Daily counts: how many clips you made, how many failed, how much you uploaded and how long rendering took, by day. They keep the daily limits, and are deleted after about a year.
  • Your clips: the newest {{KEEP_CLIPS}} you made, with their captions, so you can see, save and load them again. Older ones are deleted as new ones arrive.
  • A history of the clips you made, for 90 days: the video, its title and channel, the part you chose, the look, when, and whether it finished. It is how the History page in your account finds last month's clip.
  • Stickers you upload, and GIFs you pick, for your own clips.
  • Your GIPHY and KLIPY keys, if you add them: stored encrypted, used only for your own searches, deleted when you remove them.
  • Feedback you send from your account goes to the operator by email, with your address to reply to, and its subject and first few lines are kept with the failure reports, for 30 days. Screenshots you attach are re-encoded, with nothing but the picture kept, mailed, and not stored on the server.
  • Failure reports: when a capture, a load into WhatsApp or a render fails, the step it failed at, a short reason code and the extension and browser versions. Never the video, its title, your caption or anything you typed.
  • Server logs: the address a request came from and what it asked for, in a log of limited size that overwrites itself, to diagnose faults and stop abuse.

What is not

  • No advertising, no selling or sharing of your data, no analytics or tracking scripts, no third-party cookies set by {{NAME}}.
  • Your WhatsApp messages and contacts are never read. The extension touches WhatsApp Web only to place a clip in your Status composer when you press Load into WhatsApp, and never presses Send.
  • The extension reads a YouTube video only when you make a clip of it, only the window you chose, in your own browser.
  • The media read for a clip is deleted from the server as soon as the clip is made, or within an hour if it never is.

Google user data

{{NAME}} asks Google only for the basic sign-in scopes: openid, email and profile. They are used only to know who you are and whether you have been let in. They are not shared, sold or used for advertising, and no person reads them except to look after your access. {{NAME}}'s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who else is involved

  • Google, for signing in.
  • Cloudflare, which carries the site's traffic and runs the check on the access form that you are a person (Turnstile).
  • Resend and Google Chat, which deliver the operator a message with what you wrote when you ask for access. Nothing is ever emailed to you by {{NAME}}.
  • GIPHY or KLIPY, only if you add your own key: your GIF searches go to them, through the {{NAME}} server.

Cookies

One cookie, snipshare_session, keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It is set only when you sign in, and removed when you sign out. Google's sign-in button and Cloudflare's check may set their own cookies on their own domains while you use them.

How long, and deleting it

  • Delete my account, inside {{NAME}} under Account, removes your account, sessions, clips, history, stickers, keys and counts at once.
  • If you do not sign in for 90 days, your clips are deleted; your account stays until you or the operator removes it.
  • Failure reports are kept for 30 days. Backups of the account database are kept for 7 days.
  • To ask what is held about you, or for anything on this page, write to {{CONTACT}}.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, the change and its date are shown here, and in {{NAME}} the next time you sign in.
