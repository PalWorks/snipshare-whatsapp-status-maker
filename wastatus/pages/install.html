Get the extension

Making a clip needs Google Chrome on a computer (Windows, Mac, Linux or ChromeOS) with the {{NAME}} extension. It reads the part of the video you choose in your own browser, and places the finished clip in WhatsApp Web when you ask it to.

Download the extension

A .zip file of about 100 kB.

Its SHA-256, to check the download: {{EXTENSION_DIGEST}}

Add it to Chrome

It is not in the Chrome Web Store yet, so it is added this way. It takes a minute, once.

  1. Unzip it

    Unzip the file into a folder you will keep. Chrome reads the extension from that folder, so do not delete it afterwards.

  2. Turn on Developer mode

    Open chrome://extensions in Chrome and switch on Developer mode, at the top right.

  3. Load it, and pin it

    Press Load unpacked and choose the folder. Then pin {{NAME}} from the puzzle piece beside the address bar. Pressing it opens {{NAME}}.

Chrome may remind you now and then that a developer extension is on. That is expected for an extension added this way.

What it can reach, and why

  • YouTube: to read the window you choose, from a video playing in your own browser. Ads play as usual.
  • YouTube's thumbnails: for the artwork card.
  • WhatsApp Web: to place the clip and caption in your Status composer when you press Load into WhatsApp. It never presses Send.
  • {{NAME}}: to send the window to the renderer and fetch the finished clip.
